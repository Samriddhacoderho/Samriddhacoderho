## Selected Projects

###  Sajilo Darta — Agentic Driving-Licence Booking Automation
An agentic workflow built with **LangGraph, Python, FastAPI, Hugging Face, and OCR** to automate parts of Nepal's driving-licence booking process. Includes extraction of 16+ document fields, persistent workflow state, and human approval checkpoints before consequential actions.

###  Sahayak — Voice-First AI Banking Assistant
A **multilingual Nepali, English, and Nepglish** banking assistant using RAG to ground responses in bank-specific context. Combines a FastAPI backend with speech-recognition fine-tuning and feedback-oriented improvements.

###  Merseyside Fare-Aware Service Reliability Audit
A PySpark pipeline that processes approximately **1.07 million records** from multiple sources, applies documented data-cleaning and imputation logic, benchmarks classification models, and presents results through a Streamlit dashboard. The best reported model achieved **0.758 F1 and 0.835 ROC-AUC**.

###  Neural-Network Symmetry Research
An independent research paper examining why zero-weight initialisation can cause symmetry collapse or learning deadlock, supported by chain-rule gradient derivations and TensorFlow/Keras experiments.
